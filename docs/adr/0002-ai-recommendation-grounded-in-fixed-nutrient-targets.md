# AI Recommendations are grounded in fixed Nutrient Targets, not freely judged

The Day Summary's AI Recommendation explains and suggests foods based on comparing logged intake against each Nutrient Target, but the Nutrient Targets themselves come from RDA-based values (adjustable per Account), never from the AI's own judgment. We considered letting AI reason freely over a Day's log and decide what "lacking" or "excessive" means case by case, which would be more flexible, but it would make the verdict inconsistent between Days and unexplainable — an Account couldn't tell whether a change in feedback was due to their eating or the AI's mood. Fixing the targets keeps the verdict reproducible and lets the AI's role stay narrow: explain a computed gap and suggest foods to close it.
